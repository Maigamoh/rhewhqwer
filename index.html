Method & limits

  • Clinical data is scored with NEWS2 (Royal College of Physicians), a published, auditable early-warning score. Each point is explained per case.
  • An imaging score ≥ 0.70 escalates a case by one tier. It is shown separately and never replaces clinical judgement.
  • NEWS2 is not validated for children, pregnancy, or chronic hypercapnic respiratory failure (SpO₂ scale 2 is not applied).
  • Before real use you need local validation, bias and subgroup checks, regulatory clearance as software as a medical device, and a clinical safety case.
  • The demo data is synthetic and fully legal to use. Real patients are never needed to try this.
